# 04 · Study the algorithm in your niche

**Why it works:** posting into a feed whose rules you never studied is guesswork. Fable can run a deep research pass on your platform and niche: what formats currently earn distribution, what kills reach, how small accounts get tested, and hand you a playbook with the proof attached.

**Prompt:**

```
Run deep research on how the [Instagram] algorithm treats my niche right now.

My niche: [one line]
My account size: [rough follower count, so the advice fits my stage]

Process:
1. Research wide: current platform documentation, creator breakdowns, and recent analyses of how distribution works in my niche this year. Prefer recent sources; the rules drift.
2. Separate mechanics from myths. For every claim, note the source and how confident you are. Anything repeated everywhere but evidenced nowhere goes on a myths list.
3. Focus on my stage: how new or small accounts get tested, what signals matter in the first hours, and which formats in my niche currently earn reach versus quietly die.
4. Turn it into a one page playbook: the five rules that matter most for me, each with the proof behind it and what it changes about how I post.
5. Add a testing protocol: how I verify each rule on my own account, what to watch, and when to call a rule confirmed or busted for me.

Rules: proof over folklore. If the evidence is thin, say so instead of dressing it up. No promises about results, just the rules and how to test them.
```

**Fill in:** the platform, your niche, your account size.

**Pro tip:** rerun this once a quarter. Platform rules drift, and a playbook with a date on it beats a stale one you trust too long.

**What you keep:** the playbook plus a testing protocol you can rerun on any model.
