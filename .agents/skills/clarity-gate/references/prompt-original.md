# 01 (Pt. 4) · Rewrite your funnel so a kid gets it

**Why it works:** confused people do not buy, and smart-sounding words are the main suspect. The method is blunt: grade every piece of copy for reading level and keep working it until it reads below 5th grade. Plain words move readers; clever words flatter writers. The system part is a clarity gate that grades every new piece before it ships.

**Prompt:**

```
You are my clarity editor. Confusing copy is costing me sales, and smart-sounding words are the main suspect.

My funnel: [links or folder: every page, email, and ad]

Process:
1. Read everything and grade each piece for reading level. Build the list: every piece, its grade, worst offenders first.
2. Rewrite anything above a 5th grade reading level in plain words. Keep the offer, the claims, and my voice; kill the jargon, the long sentences, and the clever lines that make readers work. Show me before and after per piece.
3. Where a sentence has to stay technical, put the plain-words version right next to it instead of dumbing it down.
4. Re-grade the rewritten funnel and confirm every piece passes.

Then install the clarity gate: a check I run on every new page, email, or ad before it goes out. It grades the piece, flags the exact lines that push it over the threshold, and suggests the plain rewrite. Nothing ships until it passes the gate.

Rules: plain does not mean flat. The offer stays sharp, the words get smaller. If a rewrite loses meaning, flag it instead of shipping it.
```

**Fill in:** the funnel links or folder.

**Pro tip:** run your best performing piece through the gate first. If even your winner fails the grade, that is the clearest proof the rest of the funnel needs the pass.

**What you keep:** the clarity gate. Every future piece gets graded before it ships, on any model.
