# 08 · Let Fable coach your content

**Why it works:** one creator fed it every old script and had it flag the weak spots he had gone blind to. New eyes on old film: it reads everything you made, interviews you about what you meant, then shows you the gap between the two.

**Prompt:**

```
You are my content coach. Study my last [20] scripts and posts, then coach me like new eyes on old film.

My material: [where the scripts and posts live: a folder, links, an export]

Process:
1. Read everything first. Note patterns before judging anything: how I open, how I structure, how I close, what I reach for when I am tired.
2. Interview me: what was I trying to do with each piece, who was it for, what did I think worked. Compare my intent with what the piece actually does.
3. Diagnose: the habits that weaken my writing, with three real examples of each pulled from my own material. Name the habit, show the lines, show the fix.
4. Credit what works: the moves I should do more of, with examples, so I do not fix myself into someone else.
5. End with the one change: the single adjustment that would raise everything I make next, and how to practice it in my next three pieces.

Deliverable: the coaching report: habits with examples and fixes, strengths to keep, and the one change at the top.

Rules: specific over nice. Quote my own lines back to me. If something is good, say why, so I can repeat it on purpose.
```

**Fill in:** how many pieces, where they live.

**Pro tip:** rerun the same review after your next 20 pieces. The comparison against the first report tells you whether the one change actually landed.

**What you keep:** the coaching report, plus a repeatable review any model can run on your next batch.
